## Airbnb Performance Dashboard – Power BI


📌 Project Overview
An interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing Airbnb's global performance across 
10 cities. It covers the growth of Airbnb listings over time, market share by city, 
Superhost presence, average price by room type, and guest ratings.


🎯 Objectives
- Track how Airbnb's new listings evolved from its introduction to the COVID-19 period
- Identify which cities dominate the market in listings and reviews
- Compare average prices across room types
- Evaluate guest satisfaction by city across multiple rating categories


🔍 Key Insights
- 📈 Airbnb reached its **highest number of new listings in 2015**.
- 📉 **2016 and 2017** saw a slowdown, partly due to tightening local regulations.
- 💰 Despite the slowdown, Airbnb became **profitable in the second half of 2016**, 
  and 2017 was its first full year generating income.
- 🦠 After renewed growth from 2018, momentum stopped in 2019 due to the **COVID-19 pandemic**.
- 🏙️ **Paris, New York, and Sydney** account for almost half of total listings 
  and **48% of total reviews**.
- 🇫🇷 **Paris** has the most listings and reviews. A possible driver is that 
  hotel room prices are about twice as high as Airbnb prices.
- 🛏️ Average price by room type: **Hotel Room $800**, **Entire Place $673**, 
  **Shared Room $580**, **Private Room $462**.
- ⭐ Ratings are consistently high across cities, with variation by category 
  (e.g., Mexico City and Cape Town score strongly across most categories).


 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used
- **Power BI Desktop**
- **Power Query** – data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – calculated measures
- **Data Modeling** – relationships between tables
- **Data Visualization** – Pareto chart, line chart, heatmap/matrix, KPI cards, 
  bookmarks/buttons for navigation
